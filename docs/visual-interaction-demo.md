# Classroom walkthrough

Use the full tournament and all ten indicators. Start in **Explore** at `#/styles` with the team pickers empty. Allow about seven minutes. The same steps work with a pair chosen by the audience.

| Time | Action | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–1:00 | Hover or focus Ecuador on the map. Brush a few nearby points, watch the heatmap and team count change, then press Esc to bring all 48 back. | The scatterplot supplies spatial context. The heatmap reveals the actual indicators behind a nearby group. A hover or an unfinished rectangle leaves the shared link alone. |
| 1:00–2:00 | Pin Ecuador as A. Focus the first team beside **Closest to A** to locate the United States on the map and heatmap. Select it to pin B. | The United States ranks first for Ecuador. Their percentile is 99.7 against 1,128 unordered pairs. Ecuador averaged −0.50 net goals across four matches, the United States +0.60 across five. Keep Ecuador as A so the gap reads −1.10 goals per match. |
| 2:00–3:15 | Open **Compare** and switch between **Absolute profiles** and **Difference view**. Sort by gap and inspect Long balls & counters, then Press after loss. | Their largest indicator gap is only about +0.44 standard deviations for Long balls & counters. Press after loss differs by about +0.008. Compare both raw means before interpreting a small difference. |
| 3:15–4:45 | Open **Explain**. In the waterfall, select **Finishing vs xG** and then **Conceding vs xG**. Follow the connectors and compare with the observed total. | The finishing term contributes −1.896 goals per match to ECU minus USA, while the goals-prevention residual contributes about +0.914. The terms partly offset. These are retrospective model quantities, sensitive to a handful of matches. |
| 4:45–5:45 | Return to **Finishing vs xG** and expand **Inspect contributing matches**. Select Ecuador’s M34 against Curaçao to highlight its row under **Match evidence**. | Ecuador scored zero from 2.76 xG in that match. Compare with its other three entries before interpreting its mean. Each team’s own match count is the denominator. |
| 5:45–7:15 | Open **Compare** and deselect Press after loss beside the ranking. Reselect it, change scope to Group stage, then return to **Explain**. | Omitting that indicator moves the United States from first to second for Ecuador, while the full-tournament outcome accounting stays fixed. Changing scope changes the records as well: the group-stage GD gap is −1.33 and the percentile is 97.2. The same fitted model underlies both scopes. |

For open exploration, return to **Explore** and switch the map to **Team pairs**. The example region combines a similarity percentile of at least 90 with an absolute gap of at least one goal per match. It contains 53 pairs in the full dataset with all indicators. Open one from the list, or draw a different region to ask a different question.
