# Classroom walkthrough

Use the full tournament and all ten indicators. Start at `#/styles` with the team pickers empty. This walkthrough takes about seven minutes; the viewer can substitute any other pair.

| Time | Action | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–1:00 | Hover or focus Ecuador on the map. Brush a few nearby points, watch the matrix and team collection update, then press Esc. | The scatterplot supplies spatial context. The heatmap reveals the actual indicators behind a nearby group. A hover or an unfinished rectangle leaves the shared link alone. |
| 1:00–2:00 | Pin Ecuador as A. Inspect the United States from the map or the first row of Similar teams, then pin it as B. | The United States ranks first for Ecuador. Their percentile is 99.7 against 1,128 unordered pairs. Ecuador averaged −0.50 net goals across four matches, the United States +0.60 across five. Keep Ecuador as A so the gap reads −1.10 goals per match. |
| 2:00–3:15 | Switch between Absolute profiles and Difference view. Sort by gap and inspect Long balls & counters, then Press after loss. | Their largest indicator gap is only about +0.44 standard deviations for Long balls & counters. Press after loss differs by about +0.008. Compare both raw means before interpreting a small difference. |
| 3:15–4:45 | In Outcome gap waterfall, select Finishing, then the goals-prevention residual. Follow the connectors and compare with the observed total. | The finishing term contributes −1.896 goals per match to ECU minus USA, while the goals-prevention residual contributes about +0.914. The terms partly offset. These are retrospective model quantities, sensitive to a handful of matches. |
| 4:45–5:45 | With Finishing selected, open its contributing matches. Inspect Ecuador’s M34 against Curaçao and open the match evidence. | Ecuador scored zero from 2.76 xG in that match. Compare with its other three entries before interpreting its mean. Each team’s own match count is the denominator. |
| 5:45–7:15 | Deselect Press after loss in the similarity controls, then reselect it. Change scope to the group stage. | Omitting that indicator moves the United States from first to second for Ecuador, while the full-tournament outcome accounting stays fixed. Changing scope changes the records as well: the group-stage GD gap is −1.33 and the percentile is 97.2. The same fitted model underlies both scopes. |

For open exploration, switch the map to Team pairs. The example region combines a similarity percentile of at least 90 with an absolute gap of at least one goal per match. It contains 53 pairs in the full dataset with all indicators. Open one from the list, or draw a different region to ask a different question.
