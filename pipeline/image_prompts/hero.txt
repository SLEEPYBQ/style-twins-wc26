"Style Twins". Two identical generic footballers standing back-to-back in mirrored stances, same plain kit (white shirt with navy collar, navy shorts, white socks), one football at their feet between them. The LEFT twin is triumphant: both arms raised high, chest out, head tilted up, a small burst of colourful confetti above. The RIGHT twin is crestfallen: shoulders slumped, head bowed, arms hanging, a tiny grey rain cloud with three drops above the head. The symmetric composition reads as "same style, different fate".
